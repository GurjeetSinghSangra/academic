01 About

I am a PhD candidate in the Data Mining and Machine Learning (DMML) group at the University of Geneva, supervised by Prof. Alexandros Kalousis and Prof. Stéphane Marchand-Maillet.

My work sits between dynamical systems, conditional generative models and simulation-based (amortised) inference for inverse problems. Recently I have been applying these ideas to weather and climate modelling. I am also a data scientist: turning raw data into meaningful, structured information that supports decisions.

My PhD is funded by MIGRATE, a geothermal-exploration project bridging seismology and machine learning. Before Geneva, I did my MSc in Data Science between the University of Padova and the University of Helsinki, where I wrote my thesis with Luigi Acerbi and developed PyBADS.

02 Research

Grey-box dynamical systems

Hybrid models that combine incomplete ODE/PDE physics with neural components, staying interpretable while matching real observations.

Conditional generative models

Flow matching and diffusion models as simulation-free learners of stochastic, multi-modal dynamics.

Simulation-based inference

Amortised posterior estimation for inverse problems — including inference under model misspecification.

Weather & climate

Physics-informed flow matching for stable, long-horizon forecasting on ERA5.
